## Intake captured (from the working session that preceded this run — to be confirmed/corrected by Josie)
- Site already renders a **Protection** section on the location page (`location-page-body.tsx`, "ZONE 2"), showing a `Blue Park · <level>` badge + a fishing-pressure badge. Driven by `blue-parks.json` + `reef-pressure.json`.

- Verified data points available for Torre (each checkable to a named source):
  - Fisher earnings **$210 → $10,500 / day** (Marine Conservation Institute)
  - Protected area **22 → 90+ km²** today + planned expansion (MCI)
  - **3 to 7× denser white seabream** inside no-take vs outside (Melià et al. 2020, PLoS ONE) — Torre-specific
  - **~8× total fish biomass** in well-enforced Med reserves vs fished coast (Guidetti et al. 2014) — REGIONAL mean, not Torre-specific; already on-page in reef-state prose, honestly caveated
  - Blue Park level + award year + 5-year renewal (MCI / Blue Parks)
- Known tension surfaced in session: a data-chip display of outcomes **duplicated** the seabream figure that already lives in the reef-state prose (`manualReefStateBasis` in `reef-pressure.json`). Reconciliation is an open design question.
- Josie's stated preference during the session: **"just extract data and display"** — data-forward, not text-heavy.

## Decisions
- **Takeaway (the one thing):** "protection actually works." The whole block serves this.
- **Audience:** both — divers choosing a reef AND funders/partners. Same display serves both (no audience toggle stated).
- **Text budget:** data-forward, but a *little* connective language is allowed if it earns its place.
- **Reusability:** design the **reusable pattern** every Blue Park / MPA reef inherits (not a Torre one-off). Must degrade from many outcomes → one → badge-only.
- **Feeling / tone:** quietly credible, understated. Not hype, not a pride wall.

- **Numbers live in ONE home:** the protection block is the single source of truth for outcome figures. Reef-state prose gets lighter and stops repeating them (removes the seabream duplication).
- **Ecological proof leads:** the "fish came back" numbers (seabream density / fish biomass) lead the block; economic + area outcomes follow.
- **Form-factor:** responsive web, **mobile primary**, desktop as the wider canvas.

## Open questions
- Outcome hierarchy WITHIN ecological: seabream (Torre-specific) vs biomass (regional) — which is the lead stat.
- Reusable edges: reef with badge-only, 1 outcome, many; mixed sources; non-Blue-Park MPA.
- How light the reef-state prose goes once numbers move out.

## Directions rendered (for Josie to pick)
- A — quiet stat ledger; B — lead proof + supporting row; C — before→after comparison motif. (2026-07-09)

## Decisions (cont.)
- **Chosen direction: B — lead proof + supporting.**
- **Hero stat = white seabream 3 to 7× (Melià 2020).** Torre-specific and honest; preferred over the punchier but regional ~8× biomass. Biomass stays as a supporting ecological stat, clearly labelled as a Mediterranean-reserve figure.
- **Fisher earnings + protected area move OUT of the stat row and INTO a short connective sentence** (text body) below the stats. Rationale: keeps the *stats* purely on the ecological "protection works" proof; and softening the eye-popping $10,500 into prose reads as more quietly-credible than a big stat chip. Consistent with "one home" (still inside the protection block) + "a little connective language allowed."
- Net block order: badge → hero fish stat → one biomass stat → one connective sentence (economics + area) with source.
- **Citations move to an info modal.** The block carries NO inline source links — a single info affordance (ⓘ, reusing the existing `InfoButton`/`setInfo` modal pattern on the location page) opens a "where these numbers come from" popup. Consistent with the house rule "info popups, not page jumps" [[feedback_ui_principles]].
- **The modal is where the honest rigor lives:** per-figure citation + link, plus the caveats (seabream = Torre-specific density, Melià 2020; biomass ~8× = Mediterranean-reserve group mean, NOT Torre-specific, Guidetti 2014; earnings/area = MCI), and a link to the /data method page. Keeps the visible block quietly credible while the proof is one tap away.

## PIVOT (2026-07-09) — no separate protection block
Josie reversed the separate-block direction after seeing the reef-state text on the live page. Final decision:
- **No separate protection-outcome block.** All outcome points live INSIDE the reef-state basis text (`manualReefStateBasis` in `reef-pressure.json`), which already renders as the reef verdict paragraph.
- The text keeps: reef state ("Strongly protected and recovering"), seabream 3–7× denser, fish biomass ~8×, Blue Park Award.
- **Make the seabream 3–7× and the ~8× biomass more prominent** ("more prevalent") within the text.
- **Add** the protected area (22 → 90+ km²) and the fisher-earnings increase ($210 → $10,500) into the same text.
- **Citations move into the reef-state sources** (reuse `manualReefStateSourceIds` / `reefStateSources`), not inline and not a bespoke modal.
- Superseded: the blue-parks `outcomes` array + chip render, the standalone `/protection` page, the nav tab. All to be removed.
