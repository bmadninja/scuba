# PRD Quality Review — Reef-State Model Redesign

## Overall verdict

This is a genuinely strong PRD in craft — a specific, non-swappable Vision, a coherent multi-pillar thesis grounded in real comparable indices, testable FR consequences that cite the exact code invariants they preserve (verified accurate at `reef-state.ts:88`, `:106`, `atlas-location.ts:214`), honest Non-Goals, and counter-metrics that guard the thesis against gaming. What is at risk is the load-bearing joint of the whole argument: the claim — repeated in the Vision, FR-10, SM-1, and addendum §A — that the three fish-driven `thriving` overrides "have direct fish-series coverage" and will migrate to computed is **false against the data**. The three sites (`torre-guaceto-italy`, `abrolhos-banks`, `chumbe-island-tanzania`) have no record in any per-site fish-biomass or abundance file; their only structured fish-adjacent datum is a Blue Parks award tier (which the PRD miscalls "peer-reviewed biomass"). The Fish-Community Pillar as specified cannot fire for exactly the sites the redesign is built to retire. Fix the grounding of §4.4/FR-8/FR-10 and the Fish-pillar normalization units, and this clears the launch bar; ship it as-is and the headline promise is undeliverable.

## Decision-readiness — strong

§8 is a model decision log: six resolved decisions with the alternative named and given up (equal weights *as a documented policy choice* over learned calibration; basin-coarse fish normalization *accepted* over sub-basin; continuous DHW *over* recurrence with recurrence explicitly deferred to a v2 ETL change). The two Remaining questions are actually open — the acceptable-divergence set and whether the 2-basin benchmark is too coarse are not rhetorical. The `[NOTE FOR PM]` on the apex-proxy sits at a real tension (the most speculative piece), not a safe checkpoint. Counter-metrics (SM-C1, SM-C2) name what must *not* move.

The one soft spot is that the single riskiest decision — whether the three `thriving` overrides can be reproduced at all — is presented as *resolved* ("the primary reproduction test," SM-1: "the 3 fish-driven `thriving` overrides migrate to computed") when the data makes migration unlikely. A decision-ready PRD would surface this as the open risk it is (see Strategic coherence / Done-ness).

### Findings
- **high** Migration of the 3 `thriving` overrides is asserted, not open (§8 Resolved #1, SM-1) — The diff-first framing is good, but SM-1 and FR-10 pre-commit to the outcome ("migrate to computed") that the data can't support. *Fix:* demote to a Remaining question or reframe SM-1's primary line as "the 3 overrides are *adjudicated* by the regression diff — migrated or justified as retained," decoupling the success metric from an outcome the inputs don't guarantee.

## Substance over theater — strong

No furniture. The Vision earns every sentence: it names the exact gap (fish community "invisible" to a three-input cascade), the exact override count (4 true, 10 latent), the three specific sites, and the four real comparable indices (OHI, Healthy Reefs RHI, NOAA CRW, B/B₀) — none of which is swap-into-any-PRD boilerplate. This is a capability spec, and it resists persona theater correctly: two UJs, both load-bearing (UJ-1 is the trust/transparency surface, UJ-2 is the override-retirement thesis in narrative form), each with a named protagonist carrying context inline. The §C comparable-index grounding is real discovery, not an innovation-theater differentiation section. The sole NFR (build-time static, FR-1) is product-specific and tied to the existing read-only `src/data` model, not "must be scalable."

## Strategic coherence — adequate

The thesis is crisp and the features follow from it rather than reading as a backlog: fish community is the strongest health signal collected and it's invisible to the verdict; formalize the hand-built overrides into a first-class Fish pillar so they retire; keep the four diver-facing words unchanged so the *output* is stable while the *derivation* becomes defensible. Prioritization matches (the Fish pillar and override migration are the core; sightings are explicitly demoted to supporting; numeric-score is deferred). SMs validate the thesis (override reduction, chart-consistency, coverage lift) not vanity activity, and the counter-metrics are exactly the right guards.

The coherence crack is factual, at the thesis's linchpin. The entire arc rests on: *the evidence behind the fish overrides can be read by a machine pillar.* It can't, for the three sites that matter — their fish evidence exists only as prose in `manualReefStateBasis`, not as the `fish-biomass-series` / `reef-fish-abundance-series` / `agrra-reef-series` records the pillar is specified to consume (verified: all three return NONE across those files; only `channel-islands-usa`, the override being *retained*, has real fish-series coverage). So the narrative is coherent but bets on data that isn't there.

### Findings
- **critical** The Fish pillar cannot reproduce the 3 `thriving` overrides — the data isn't per-site (§1 Vision, §4.4 desc, FR-10, SM-1, addendum §A) — "All 4 overrides have direct fish-series coverage" is false: `torre-guaceto-italy`, `abrolhos-banks`, `chumbe-island-tanzania` appear in none of `fish-biomass-series.json`, `reef-fish-abundance-series.json`, `agrra-reef-series.json`. Their structured fish datum is a Blue Parks award tier only. The pillar as specified has no data to fire on for precisely the sites the redesign exists to retire, so under the FR-3 floor these sites would fall back to coral/thermal — the very signals that forced a manual override in the first place. *Fix:* either (a) ingest the biomass numbers cited in each `manualReefStateBasis` into `fish-biomass-series.json` as an ETL precondition (and say so in scope — currently a Non-Goal excludes ETL work), or (b) rewrite the Vision/FR-10/SM-1 to admit these three likely *stay* overrides and pick a different, data-backed primary reproduction case.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate

Per-FR, the discipline is high: FR-1 through FR-17 each carry a "Consequences (testable)" block, and the ones that preserve current behavior cite the exact line they mirror — all verified accurate (FR-3 → `reef-state.ts:88`; FR-4 → `:106`; FR-11 → `fishingAllowsImproving` = protected/low/unknown, exact; FR-16 → `atlas-location.ts:214`). Mechanics that are genuinely implementation are correctly delegated to the addendum (composite formula, tier thresholds, freshness reuse). An engineer could build FR-1–FR-7, FR-11–FR-17 from this.

The headline pillar is the least done. FR-8's testable consequence — "biomass several times the regional fished-reef baseline produces a strongly positive score" — references a **regional fished-reef biomass baseline that does not exist in the data**. `reef-check-fish-regional.json` holds indicator *densities* (individuals per 100 m²) and qualitative MPA-contrast prose, not a fished/unfished biomass reference. Per-site biomass arrives in three incompatible units: RLS `biomassKgPerHa` (kg/ha), REEF `densityIndex` (unitless ~0–5), AGRRA fish biomass, plus a global literature B₀ constant (~1,000–1,900 kg/ha) that the addendum itself flags "verify before hard-coding." No FR says how these collapse into one 0..1 Fish Pillar Score. The single most important, most novel pillar is the one whose "done" is undefined.

### Findings
- **high** FR-8 normalizes against a biomass baseline the data doesn't contain, across mismatched units (§4.4 FR-8, addendum §B) — regional file is indicator densities per 100 m², not fished/unfished biomass; per-site sources are kg/ha vs a unitless density index; B₀ is an unverified literature constant. The normalization that produces the Fish Pillar Score is unspecified and partly ungrounded. *Fix:* add an FR (or addendum sub-section) defining the per-source normalization path to a common 0..1 — e.g. RLS/AGRRA kg/ha ÷ literature B₀; REEF densityIndex mapped via its own regional reference — and state which sites have biomass-grade vs density-grade vs award-only evidence, and how confidence differs.
- **high** Blue Parks is described as "peer-reviewed biomass" but carries no biomass (§4.4 desc, FR-8, Glossary "Fish-Community Pillar", addendum §B) — `blue-parks.json` fields are `level, locationId, note, parkName, sourceIds, year`. A Blue Park award is a protection credential, not a fish-biomass measurement; feeding it into the Fish pillar as biomass double-counts the Fishing pillar's protection signal and overstates fish evidence for the 3 override sites (whose *only* structured fish-adjacent datum it is). *Fix:* reclassify Blue Parks level as a Fishing-pillar protection modifier (or a confidence input), and strike "biomass" from every reference to `blue-parks.json`.
- **medium** FR-10's "primary reproduction test" is currently a test that must fail (FR-10) — "each must compute to `thriving` via the Fish + Fishing pillars" is unsatisfiable while the Fish pillar has no per-site data for the three sites. *Fix:* fold into the critical finding above; make the test "computed verdict is adjudicated," not "computes to thriving."

## Scope honesty — strong

Non-Goals do real work and pre-empt silent assumptions: no numeric score (with `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` and a v2 pointer), no real-time, no ETL rework, no operator-editable ratings, no new charts. `[ASSUMPTION]` on the build-time-static inference is tagged and indexed (§9 roundtrips — the one inline assumption is the one indexed). `[NOTE FOR PM]` sits at the apex-proxy, the genuine soft spot. Deferred work (thermal recurrence) is logged in FR-7 not dropped. Open-items density is low and appropriate for a green-light PRD.

The honesty gap is not in the Non-Goals but in a fact stated as settled: §9 opens "All prior draft assumptions resolved by the 2026-07-09 data investigation," and §8 #6 says fish coverage is "confirmed" — yet the investigation's own conclusion ("all 4 overrides have direct fish-series coverage") is wrong, which means a resolved decision rests on an unverified data reading. That is a scope-honesty problem: the reader is told the ground is firm where it isn't.

### Findings
- **high** A "resolved by data investigation" claim is itself the ungrounded one (§8 #1 & #6, §9 header, addendum §A "Data findings") — the migration feasibility was marked resolved on a fish-coverage reading that doesn't hold. *Fix:* re-run the per-site coverage check, correct addendum §A, and move override-migration feasibility back to Remaining Questions until the biomass-ingest question is settled.
- **low** ETL Non-Goal vs the likely fix are in tension (§5) — if the three overrides need their `manualReefStateBasis` biomass ingested to migrate, that *is* ETL work the Non-Goal excludes. *Fix:* name the narrow exception explicitly if you take that path.

## Downstream usability — strong

FR IDs 1–17 are contiguous and unique; UJ-1/UJ-2, SM-1..5 plus counter-metrics all resolve; every SM cross-references the FR(s) it validates and vice versa. Each FR pulls out alone because it names its own code surface. The addendum's source-field map (§B) and implementation surface (§D) give architecture/story creation clean extraction points. This will feed downstream well.

### Findings
- **low** Region keys in addendum §A are abbreviated forms that don't match the data file keys — `etp` / `persga-red-sea` / `ropme` / `wio` vs actual `etp-eastern-tropical-pacific` / `persga-red-sea-gulf-of-aden` / `ropme-persian-arabian-gulf` / `wio-western-indian-ocean` in `coral-cover-regional.json`. *Fix:* use the literal keys so the implementer can match them directly.
- **low** Consumer-component names are inconsistent between PRD and addendum — FR-2 lists `reef-health-badge`; addendum §D lists `reef-state-card.tsx` / `reef-state-badge.tsx`. *Fix:* reconcile against the real component filenames before story creation.

## Shape fit — strong

UJ density is kept to two, both load-bearing — no over-formalization, and the reader-facing transparency surface (UJ-1) justifies the UJs that a pure internal tool might have dropped. DHW coverage (120/121) and the 4/10 override split verified against the data. Not forced into a consumer-PRD shape; not under-formalized for its stakes.
